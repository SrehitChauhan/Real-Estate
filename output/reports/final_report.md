# Market Insight Report: Real Estate Price & Neighbourhood Intelligence

## Executive Summary
This study analyses **1,241 validated property records** (after auditing 1,268 raw records and removing 18 duplicates, 5 implausible price/area entries, and 4 unmatched neighbourhood keys) across six urban and suburban neighbourhoods from **January 2024 to December 2025**. By standardising units (`sqft`), currencies (`USD`), and comparable property cohorts, the analysis isolates true spatial value drivers from listing-price inflation and compositional bias.

## 8 Evidence-Backed Findings
1. **Substantial Neighbourhood Valuation Spread**: **Greenwood Heights** commands the highest median valuation at **$437.49/sqft** (median total price **$542,217**, IQR **$94.03/sqft**), representing a **70.9% premium** over **Southgate Industrial** at **$255.93/sqft** (median total price **$323,274**, IQR **$47.72/sqft**).
2. **Proximity to CBD & Household Income Are Primary Spatial Drivers**: NumPy Pearson correlations demonstrate strong linear alignment between `price_per_sqft` and `dist_cbd_km` ($r = -0.6798$), `median_household_income` ($r = +0.6783$), and `transit_score` ($r = +0.6753$), followed by `school_rating` ($r = +0.6037$).
3. **Persistent Listing-vs-Sale Asking Bias**: Across all six neighbourhoods, active `Listing` asking prices exceed closed `Sale` transaction prices. **Oakridge Park** exhibits the widest asking premium at **+12.29%** ($424.26 vs. $377.81/sqft), followed by **Riverside Quarter** (**+9.59%**) and **Greenwood Heights** (**+7.81%**). Relying on unadjusted listing portals overstates market clearing values by ~6–12%.
4. **Robust Outlier Sensitivity Confirms Median Stability**: Using NumPy MAD-based Modified Z-scores ($|Z_{mod}| > 3.5$) and $1.5 \times \text{IQR}$ fences flags **5 upper-tail outliers**. Sensitivity testing confirms that excluding outliers shifts closed-sale medians by **0.00%** in five neighbourhoods and only **0.75%** in Greenwood Heights ($431.25 vs. $428.04/sqft), proving median PPSF is resilient to luxury tail skew.
5. **Comparable-Segment Valuation Hierarchy**: Controlling for property type and size tier reveals that **Mid-Size Condos (1,000–1,799 sqft)** achieve the highest segment median at **$398/sqft**, outperforming **Large Detached Houses (1,800+ sqft)** (**$394/sqft**) and **Compact Townhouses (<1,000 sqft)** (**$355/sqft**) due to higher concentration in Urban Core and Waterfront zones.
6. **Moderate Amenity Premium Within Segments**: Physical amenities (`has_parking`, `has_gym_pool`, `renovated`) show a positive correlation of $r = +0.1836$ with `price_per_sqft`, lifting property valuations modestly within identical neighbourhoods, whereas `property_age` alone exhibits negligible linear correlation ($r = -0.0285$) because older Urban Core units retain high location value.
7. **Steady Upward Appreciation Across 2024–2025**: Monthly resampled 3-month rolling medians show consistent appreciation across all zone types between Q1 2024 and Q4 2025, with **Urban Core** climbing from ~$397/sqft to ~$446/sqft (+12.3%) and **Transitioning** zones rising from ~$250/sqft to ~$270/sqft (+8.0%).
8. **Higher Price Dispersion in Premium Markets**: Interquartile range (`IQR`) scales directly with median price level—Greenwood Heights exhibits an IQR of **$94.03/sqft** compared to **$47.72/sqft** in Southgate Industrial—indicating greater micro-location and renovation heterogeneity in high-demand districts.

## Assumptions, Limitations & Analytical Risks
- **Listing-vs-Sale Composition Bias**: Active listings represent seller aspirations rather than cleared market value; valuation models must filter on `record_type == 'Sale'` or apply neighbourhood-specific discount factors.
- **Omitted Variable Bias**: Structural condition, floor level, view quality, HOA fees, and micro-school catchment boundaries are unobserved and contribute to within-segment residual variance.
- **Synthetic Benchmark Caveat**: While calibrated to realistic multi-currency/multi-unit distributions for reproducible execution, exact elasticity coefficients should be recalibrated before live underwriting.
